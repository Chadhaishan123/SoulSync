Immediate support

Crisis help

If you might hurt yourself or someone else, contact emergency services now or go to the nearest emergency department.

Emergency first

Call your local emergency number. In India, you can call 112 for emergency assistance.

Call 112

Right now

Move away from harm

Put distance between yourself and anything you could use to hurt yourself.

Contact a person

Call or message someone you trust and tell them you need immediate support.

Stay visible

Go to a shared room, public place, or near another person while help is arranged.

Support contacts

Emergency services

Call 112 in India, or your local emergency number if you are elsewhere.

Trusted contact

Save a friend, family member, mentor, or professional you can reach quickly.

Nearest hospital

Use emergency care when your safety cannot wait for an appointment.

Soul Sync can offer organization and wellness tools, but it cannot provide emergency intervention, diagnosis, or treatment.
