Self-guided support

Resources

Short, practical reads and exercises for anxiety, sleep, stress, and emotional awareness.

Box breathing

Inhale for four, hold for four, exhale for four, hold for four. Repeat gently.

5-4-3-2-1 grounding

Name five things you see, four you feel, three you hear, two you smell, and one you taste.

One next step

Choose the smallest useful action available: drink water, message someone, step outside, or pause.
